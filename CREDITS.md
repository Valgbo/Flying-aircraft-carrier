### Models, textures and model data

Every file below is generated from the originals by the converter. Common changes: converted
from AC3D to glTF 2.0 binary in our body frame and metres, normals computed with the AC3D crease
angles, polygons triangulated, geometry quantized (`KHR_mesh_quantization`), static parts merged
per material, animated parts wrapped in hinge pivots; textures re-encoded (JPEG, or PNG where the
alpha channel is used) and resized. "Built in code" marks geometry made by
`scripts/a10/convert-lib-build.mjs` (this project, GPL-2.0-or-later).
